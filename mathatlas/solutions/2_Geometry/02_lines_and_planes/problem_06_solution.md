# Problem Statement

**Exercise 6: Angle Between Planes.**

Given
$$\Pi_1:x+y+z=1,\qquad \Pi_2:x-y=2.$$
First use the normal vectors to decide whether the planes are parallel. If not, compute the angle between them and explain why the normal vectors alone are enough for this calculation.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

The angle between planes equals the acute angle between their normal directions.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** The angle between planes equals the acute angle between their normal directions.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** Normals $n_1=(1,1,1)$ and $n_2=(1,-1,0)$. Their dot product is $1-1+0=0$. Therefore planes are not parallel; they meet at a right angle $90^\circ$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

Both normals are nonzero and orthogonal, so their angle is $\arccos0=90^\circ$.
