# Problem Statement

**Exercise 2: A Line in Space.**

Given
$$L:(x,y,z)=(2,-1,3)+t(1,2,-2).$$
State a point on the line and its direction vector, check whether $P=(4,3,-1)$ lies on $L$, and find the point corresponding to $t=-1$.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

A parametric line consists of a starting point plus multiples of its direction vector.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** A parametric line consists of a starting point plus multiples of its direction vector.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** Point $(2,-1,3)$ and direction $(1,2,-2)$. For $P=(4,3,-1)$, coordinate differences from the base are $(2,4,-4)=2(1,2,-2)$, so $P$ lies on the line at $t=2$. At $t=-1$, point $(1,-3,5)$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

Substitution $t=2$ yields $(4,3,-1)$; $t=-1$ yields $(1,-3,5)$.
