# Problem Statement

**Exercise 9: Distances.**

Compute:
1. the distance from $P=(2,3)$ to the line $3x+4y-10=0$,
2. the distance from $Q=(2,-1,3)$ to the plane $2x-2y+z-4=0$.

Compare the two formulas and explain their common connection with the normal direction.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

Point-to-line and point-to-plane distances use absolute signed displacement divided by normal length.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** Point-to-line and point-to-plane distances use absolute signed displacement divided by normal length.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** In 2D: $d(P,3x+4y-10=0)=|3(2)+4(3)-10|/\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=8/5$. In 3D: $d(Q,2x-2y+z-4=0)=|2(2)-2(-1)+3-4|/\sqrt{2^2+(-2)^2+1^2}=5/3$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

Normal lengths are 5 and 3; the signed substitutions are 8 and 5, respectively.
