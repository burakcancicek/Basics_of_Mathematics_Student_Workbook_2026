# Problem Statement

**Exercise 1: Line Through Two Points.**

Find the equation of the line through $A=(1,2)$ and $B=(4,-1)$ in parametric and general form. Identify a direction vector and an example of a normal vector. Check that both points satisfy the equation you obtain.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

A line through two points has direction $B-A$; a normal is perpendicular to that direction.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** A line through two points has direction $B-A$; a normal is perpendicular to that direction.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $B-A=(3,-3)$, so a direction is $(1,-1)$ and a normal $(1,1)$. Parametric form $(x,y)=(1,2)+t(1,-1)$. General form $x+y=3$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

$A$: $1+2=3$; $B$: $4+(-1)=3$. The normal dot direction is $1-1=0$.
