# Problem Statement

**Exercise 4: Plane Through Three Points.**

Find the equation of the plane through
$$A=(1,0,0),\quad B=(0,1,0),\quad C=(0,0,1).$$
First construct two vectors lying in the plane, and then use the cross product. Check the resulting equation on all three points.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

Three non-collinear points determine a plane; the cross product of two in-plane vectors is normal.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** Three non-collinear points determine a plane; the cross product of two in-plane vectors is normal.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $AB=(-1,1,0)$, $AC=(-1,0,1)$; $AB\times AC=(1,1,1)$. Thus $x+y+z=1$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

At $A,B,C$, the sum of coordinates is 1 in each case. The cross product is nonzero, so the points are non-collinear.
