# Problem Statement

**Exercise 14: Contrast: Intersection and Parallelism.**

From the point $P=(0,0,1)$, two lines are drawn with direction vectors
$$v_1=(2,1,-1),\qquad v_2=(2,1,0).$$
For each line, analyze its position relative to the plane $z=0$.

For the line that intersects the plane, find the intersection point. For the other line, explain how you can recognize parallelism without solving an equation.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

A line crosses the plane $z=0$ when its $z$ coordinate can reach zero; zero vertical direction means parallel.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** A line crosses the plane $z=0$ when its $z$ coordinate can reach zero; zero vertical direction means parallel.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** For $v_1=(2,1,-1)$, line $(2t,t,1-t)$ reaches $z=0$ at $t=1$, intersection $(2,1,0)$. For $v_2=(2,1,0)$, line $(2t,t,1)$ has $z=1$ always, so it is parallel to and outside the plane.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

At $t=1$ the first line has $z=0$; the second has constant $z=1$ for every real $t$.
