# Problem Statement

**Exercise 7: A Line Relative to a Plane.**

For
$$L:(x,y,z)=(1,1,0)+t(1,-1,0),\qquad \Pi:x+y+z=2,$$
determine whether the line intersects the plane at one point, is parallel to it, or lies in it. Explain the role of the dot product of the direction vector and the normal vector.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

For a line $p+tv$ and plane $n\cdot x=d$, test $n\cdot v$ and $n\cdot p$.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** For a line $p+tv$ and plane $n\cdot x=d$, test $n\cdot v$ and $n\cdot p$.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $v=(1,-1,0)$ and $n=(1,1,1)$ give $n\cdot v=0$. The base point $(1,1,0)$ satisfies $1+1+0=2$. Hence the entire line lies in the plane.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

For every $t$, $(1+t)+(1-t)+0=2$, so every point on the line is in the plane.
