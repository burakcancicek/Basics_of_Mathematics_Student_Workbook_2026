# Problem Statement

**Exercise 3: Plane Through a Point with a Given Normal Vector.**

Find the equation of the plane through $P=(1,-2,3)$ and perpendicular to $n=(2,1,-1)$. Check the result by substituting the point $P$ and explain the role of the normal vector.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

A plane with normal $n$ through $P$ satisfies $n\cdot(X-P)=0$.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** A plane with normal $n$ through $P$ satisfies $n\cdot(X-P)=0$.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $2(x-1)+(y+2)-(z-3)=0$, simplifying to $2x+y-z+3=0$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

At $P=(1,-2,3)$, $2-2-3+3=0$. Its coefficients $(2,1,-1)$ equal the specified normal.
