# Problem Statement

**Exercise 13: Closest Point on a Plane.**

Find the point on the plane $x+2y+2z=4$ closest to $P=(3,0,0)$. Give the distance and check that the segment joining the two points is perpendicular to the plane.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

The closest point on a plane is obtained by moving along the plane's normal.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** The closest point on a plane is obtained by moving along the plane's normal.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** Plane normal $n=(1,2,2)$, $d=4$. For $P=(3,0,0)$, $n\cdot P-d=-1$ and $\|n\|^2=9$. Thus $Q=P-(-1/9)n=(28/9,2/9,2/9)$. Distance $|{-1}|/3=1/3$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

Plane check: $28/9+4/9+4/9=4$. Difference $Q-P=(1/9,2/9,2/9)=n/9$, perpendicular to the plane.
