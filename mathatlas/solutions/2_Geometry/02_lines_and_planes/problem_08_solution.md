# Problem Statement

**Exercise 8: Intersection Point of a Line and a Plane.**

Find the intersection point of
$$(x,y,z)=(1,0,2)+t(1,2,-1)$$
with the plane $x+y+z=4$. Check the result by substituting it into both descriptions.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

Insert the line coordinates into the plane equation to solve for the parameter.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** Insert the line coordinates into the plane equation to solve for the parameter.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** Line: $x=1+t$, $y=2t$, $z=2-t$. Plane: $(1+t)+2t+(2-t)=4$, so $3+2t=4$ and $t=1/2$. Intersection $(3/2,1,3/2)$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

Plane check: $3/2+1+3/2=4$. Line check: $t=1/2$ produces the same coordinates.
