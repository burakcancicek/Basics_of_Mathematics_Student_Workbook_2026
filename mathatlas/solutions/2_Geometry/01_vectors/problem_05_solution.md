# Problem Statement

**Exercise 5: Angle Between Vectors.**

Given $u=(1,1,0)$ and $v=(1,0,1)$:

1. Use the sign of $u\cdot v$ to predict the type of angle.
2. Compute the angle.
3. Compare the result with the prediction.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

Use $\cos\theta=(u\cdot v)/(\|u\|\|v\|)$.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** Use $\cos\theta=(u\cdot v)/(\|u\|\|v\|)$.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $u\cdot v=1>0$, so the angle is acute. Both lengths equal $\sqrt2$, hence $\cos\theta=1/2$ and $\theta=60^\circ=\pi/3$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

Since $\cos60^\circ=1/2>0$, the computed angle matches the predicted acute type.
