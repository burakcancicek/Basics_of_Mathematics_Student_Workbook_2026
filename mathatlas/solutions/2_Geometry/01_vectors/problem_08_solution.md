# Problem Statement

**Exercise 8: Linear Combination and Checking the Result.**

Check whether $w=(5,1)$ can be written as
$$w=au+bv,$$
where $u=(1,1)$ and $v=(2,-1)$. If so, find $a,b$, reconstruct $w$ from them, and explain the geometric meaning of the existence of such coefficients.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

Solve the two coordinate equations for the coefficients.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** Solve the two coordinate equations for the coefficients.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $a+2b=5$ and $a-b=1$. Subtracting gives $3b=4$, so $b=4/3$ and $a=7/3$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

$(7/3)(1,1)+(4/3)(2,-1)=(7/3+8/3,7/3-4/3)=(5,1)$.
