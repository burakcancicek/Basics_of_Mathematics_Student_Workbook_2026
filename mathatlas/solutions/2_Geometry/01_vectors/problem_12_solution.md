# Problem Statement

**Exercise 12: A Normal Vector to a Plane.**

Given $A=(1,0,2)$, $B=(2,1,0)$, and $C=(0,3,1)$. Construct two vectors lying in the plane, find a vector perpendicular to both, and check the perpendicularity using dot products.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

Take two vectors in the plane and cross them to obtain a normal.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** Take two vectors in the plane and cross them to obtain a normal.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $AB=(1,1,-2)$ and $AC=(-1,3,-1)$. Their cross product is $n=(5,3,4)$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

$n\cdot AB=5+3-8=0$; $n\cdot AC=-5+9-4=0$.
