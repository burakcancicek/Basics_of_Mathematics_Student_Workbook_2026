# Problem Statement

**Exercise 9: Cross Product as a Perpendicular Direction.**

For $u=(1,2,0)$ and $v=(0,1,3)$ compute $u\times v$. Use dot products to check perpendicularity to both vectors, compute $v\times u$, and compare the results.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

The cross product in three dimensions is perpendicular to both input vectors.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** The cross product in three dimensions is perpendicular to both input vectors.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $u\times v=(2\cdot3-0\cdot1,\ 0\cdot0-1\cdot3,\ 1\cdot1-2\cdot0)=(6,-3,1)$. Also $v\times u=(-6,3,-1)$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

$(6,-3,1)\cdot(1,2,0)=6-6=0$; $(6,-3,1)\cdot(0,1,3)=-3+3=0$.
