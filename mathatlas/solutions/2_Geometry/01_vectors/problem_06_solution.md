# Problem Statement

**Exercise 6: Projection and Perpendicular Component.**

Find the projection of $u=(4,2)$ onto the direction $v=(1,1)$. Then compute
$$r=u-\operatorname{proj}_v u$$
and verify that $r\cdot v=0$. Explain this result geometrically.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

The projection of $u$ on $v$ is $(u\cdot v)/(v\cdot v)\,v$.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** The projection of $u$ on $v$ is $(u\cdot v)/(v\cdot v)\,v$.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $u\cdot v=6$, $v\cdot v=2$, so $\operatorname{proj}_v u=3(1,1)=(3,3)$. The remainder is $r=(4,2)-(3,3)=(1,-1)$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

$r\cdot v=1-1=0$ and $(3,3)+(1,-1)=(4,2)$.
