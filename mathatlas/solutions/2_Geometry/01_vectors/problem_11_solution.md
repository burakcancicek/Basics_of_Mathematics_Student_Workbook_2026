# Problem Statement

**Exercise 11: Decomposition into Parallel and Perpendicular Components.**

Decompose $u=(3,4)$ into components parallel and perpendicular to $v=(1,2)$. Check
$$u=u_{\parallel}+u_{\perp},\qquad u_{\perp}\cdot v=0.$$
Explain why these are two independent checks of correctness.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

Orthogonal decomposition separates a vector into a part along $v$ and a perpendicular remainder.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** Orthogonal decomposition separates a vector into a part along $v$ and a perpendicular remainder.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $u\cdot v=11$, $v\cdot v=5$. Thus $u_{\parallel}=(11/5,22/5)$ and $u_{\perp}=(3,4)-(11/5,22/5)=(4/5,-2/5)$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

Sum is $(15/5,20/5)=(3,4)$; dot product $(4/5)(1)+(-2/5)(2)=0$. The sum and perpendicularity are independent checks.
