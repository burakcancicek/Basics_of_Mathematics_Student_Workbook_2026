# Problem Statement

**Exercise 1: Vector Operations and Their Meaning.**

For $u=(2,-1,3)$ and $v=(-1,4,2)$ compute
$$u+v,\qquad u-v,\qquad 3u-2v.$$
Then verify that $(u+v)-v=u$, and explain geometrically what adding two vectors means.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

Add and subtract vectors component by component.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** Add and subtract vectors component by component.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $u+v=(1,3,5)$, $u-v=(3,-5,1)$, $3u-2v=(8,-11,5)$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

$(u+v)-v=(1,3,5)-(-1,4,2)=(2,-1,3)=u$. Geometrically addition places arrows head-to-tail.
