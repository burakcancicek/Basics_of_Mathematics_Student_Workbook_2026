# Problem Statement

**Exercise 3: Vector Normalization.**

For $v=(3,4)$ find a unit vector $e$ with the same direction and orientation. Check that $\|e\|=1$, reconstruct $v$ in the form $v=\|v\|e$, and explain what changes during normalization and what remains unchanged.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

Normalize by dividing each coordinate by the vector's length.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** Normalize by dividing each coordinate by the vector's length.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $\|v\|=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$. Hence $e=v/5=(3/5,4/5)$. Reconstruct $v=5e=(3,4)$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

$\|e\|=\sqrt{9/25+16/25}=1$. The direction stays the same because the scaling factor $1/5$ is positive.
