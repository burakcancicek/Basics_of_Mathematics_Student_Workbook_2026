# Problem Statement

**Exercise 10: Triangle Area from Vectors.**

The points $A=(0,0,0)$, $B=(2,1,0)$, and $C=(1,3,2)$ form a triangle. Construct $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AC}$, compute the area of the parallelogram using the cross product, and then find the area of the triangle. Explain the factor $\frac12$.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

A cross product magnitude is the area of the parallelogram spanned by two sides.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** A cross product magnitude is the area of the parallelogram spanned by two sides.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $AB=(2,1,0)$ and $AC=(1,3,2)$. $AB\times AC=(2,-4,5)$, with norm $\sqrt{4+16+25}=\sqrt{45}=3\sqrt5$. Triangle area $=3\sqrt5/2$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

A diagonal divides a parallelogram into two congruent triangles, hence the factor $1/2$.
