# Problem Statement

**Exercise 4: Powers and Oscillation.**

Analyze
$$\left(\frac23\right)^n,\qquad \left(\frac32\right)^n,\qquad (-1)^n,\qquad \frac{(-1)^n}{n}.$$
For each sequence, describe the type of behavior and justify your answer.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

Geometric powers shrink if $|r|<1$, grow if $r>1$, and may oscillate for negative bases.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** Geometric powers shrink if $|r|<1$, grow if $r>1$, and may oscillate for negative bases.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $(2/3)^n\to0$; $(3/2)^n\to+\infty$; $(-1)^n$ alternates between $1$ and $-1$ and has no limit; $(-1)^n/n\to0$ by $|(-1)^n/n|\le1/n$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

Even and odd subsequences of $(-1)^n$ have different limits, while the oscillating fraction is squeezed between $-1/n$ and $1/n$.
