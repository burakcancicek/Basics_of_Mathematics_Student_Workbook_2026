# Problem Statement

**Exercise 2: Different Growth Rates.**

Compute
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{3n+1}{n^2+4},\qquad \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{2n^3-n}{5n^2+1}.$$
Compare the two cases and formulate a general observation about the degrees of the polynomials in the numerator and denominator.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

Compare the degrees of numerator and denominator.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** Compare the degrees of numerator and denominator.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $\frac{3n+1}{n^2+4}=\frac{3/n+1/n^2}{1+4/n^2}\to0$. Meanwhile $\frac{2n^3-n}{5n^2+1}=n\frac{2-1/n^2}{5+1/n^2}\to+\infty$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

The first ratio is asymptotic to $3/n$; the second to $2n/5$.
