# Problem Statement

**Exercise 12: Exponential Function Limit.**

Compute
$$\lim_{x\to0}(1+3x)^{1/x}.$$
Connect the result with the limit from Exercise 5 and show the appropriate transformation.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

Substitute $t=3x$ and use the standard exponential limit.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** Substitute $t=3x$ and use the standard exponential limit.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $(1+3x)^{1/x}=((1+t)^{1/t})^3\to e^3$ as $t=3x\to0$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

Taking logarithms yields $\ln L=\lim_{x\to0}\ln(1+3x)/x=3$, so $L=e^3$.
