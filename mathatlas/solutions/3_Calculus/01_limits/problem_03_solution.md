# Problem Statement

**Exercise 3: A Square Root and Removing an Indeterminate Form.**

Compute
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(\sqrt{n^2+5n}-n\right).$$
Use the conjugate expression and explain why this transformation removes the difficulty.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

Rationalize by multiplying by the conjugate.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** Rationalize by multiplying by the conjugate.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $\sqrt{n^2+5n}-n=\frac{(n^2+5n)-n^2}{\sqrt{n^2+5n}+n}=\frac5{\sqrt{1+5/n}+1}\to\frac52$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

The denominator approaches $1+1=2$ after dividing by $n$.
