# Problem Statement

**Exercise 10: Continuity of a Piecewise Function.**

Find $a$ so that
$$f(x)=\begin{cases}ax+1,&x<2,\\x^2-1,&x\ge2\end{cases}$$
is continuous at $x=2$. Explicitly compare the left-hand limit, right-hand limit, and function value.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

Continuity at the joining point requires the left limit to equal the value and right limit.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** Continuity at the joining point requires the left limit to equal the value and right limit.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** Left limit at 2 is $2a+1$. Right limit and $f(2)$ are $2^2-1=3$. Set $2a+1=3$, obtaining $a=1$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

With $a=1$, left side tends to 3 and right side tends to 3; the function value is 3.
