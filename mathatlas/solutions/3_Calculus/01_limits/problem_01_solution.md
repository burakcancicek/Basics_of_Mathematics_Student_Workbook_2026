# Problem Statement

**Exercise 1: Dominant Terms in a Sequence.**

Compute
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{4n^2-3n+1}{2n^2+5n-7}.$$
Explain why the highest-degree terms determine the result.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

Divide numerator and denominator by the highest power, $n^2$.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the method.** Divide numerator and denominator by the highest power, $n^2$.

**Step 2 — Calculate and interpret.** $\frac{4-3/n+1/n^2}{2+5/n-7/n^2}\to\frac42=2$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

Every term containing $1/n$ or $1/n^2$ tends to zero; denominator tends to 2, not 0.
